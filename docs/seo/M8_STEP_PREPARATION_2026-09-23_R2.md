If relation is:

- adjacent task;
- variant with uncertain referent;
- different marketplace;
- different entity;
- different search intent;
- different product capability boundary;

then identities remain separate and relation is recorded.

### Deterministic semantic identity ID

For identities backed by raw occurrences:

1. collect sorted unique member `exact_safe_key` values;
2. join with newline;
3. SHA-256 the resulting UTF-8 string;
4. semantic identity ID:
   `M8SID_<first 16 lowercase hex chars>`.

For product-only BRAND_DEFENSE without raw occurrence:

hash:
`BRAND_DEFENSE\n<exact_safe_brand_key>`.

This makes identity IDs membership-sensitive and reproducible.
